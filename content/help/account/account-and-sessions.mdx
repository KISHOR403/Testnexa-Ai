# Managing your account and workspace

Testnexa organizes all suites, test cases, integrations, and coverage metrics inside isolated workspaces. You can manage multiple team workspaces under a single user identity.

> Note: User sessions are secured via industry-standard JSON Web Tokens with strict expiration windows. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and OAuth providers (Google, GitHub) are fully supported.

## Workspace roles & permissions

Workspaces enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

- **Owner**: Full administrative authority, billing controls, workspace renaming, and deletion.
- **Admin**: Can invite members, configure GitHub integrations, and manage suites.
- **Member**: Can generate test suites, review and approve test cases, and export tests.
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to suites, coverage dashboards, and risk matrices.

## How to invite team members

1. Navigate to **App Settings** from the sidebar or click your user avatar in the bottom navigation.
2. Select the **Team & Members** tab.
3. Click **Invite Member** and enter your colleague's professional email address.
4. Assign their role (**Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer**) and send the invitation.
5. The invitee receives a secure single-use registration link valid for 48 hours.

## Expected result

Invited team members gain access to shared workspace suites, facilitating collaborative test authoring and synchronized coverage metrics.

## Guidance & best practices

> Tip: If you frequently work across multiple projects or clients, create dedicated workspaces for each repository to keep requirement sets and metrics completely isolated.

> Warning: Removing a member immediately revokes their active session and invalidates all in-flight requests made with their tokens.
