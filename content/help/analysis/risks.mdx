# Analyzing requirements risks

Testnexa analyzes your product requirements and tests to identify potential failure modes, categorizing them across a standard 5×5 Likelihood and Impact matrix. This analysis surfaces severe vulnerabilities before code reaches production environments.

> Note: Risks are derived automatically from your negative, edge, and non-functional test cases. Each risk item highlights the severity score and the specific test cases designed to mitigate it.

## The 5×5 risk matrix

Risk scores are computed by multiplying the estimated Likelihood of occurrence (1 to 5) by the operational Impact (1 to 5):

- **Critical (Score 20–25)**: High probability of widespread outage, data breach, or payment failure. Demands immediate test coverage and verification.
- **High (Score 12–19)**: Significant degradation affecting core user journeys or critical integrations.
- **Medium (Score 6–11)**: Edge case errors, validation omissions, or localized UI inconsistencies.
- **Low (Score 1–5)**: Minor cosmetic quirks or rare corner conditions with minimal impact.

## How to prioritize risks

1. **Open the Risks dashboard**
   Navigate to **Analysis > Risks** from the sidebar.
2. **Filter by the 5×5 grid**
   Click any cell in the 5×5 matrix to filter the risk inventory by that specific Likelihood and Impact combination.
3. **Inspect mitigation status**
   Review each risk card to verify whether test cases exist to detect that failure vector. Unmitigated critical risks are flagged immediately.
4. **Export risk summary**
   Use the **Export risks** action to download a formatted CSV or JSON report for QA audit compliance and sprint planning.

## Expected result

Your team gains a prioritized backlog of high-risk scenarios and clear visibility into which threats lack defensive automated tests.

## Guidance & best practices

> Tip: Filter by the top-right quadrant (Likelihood 4–5 and Impact 4–5) during sprint planning to ensure critical payment and authentication paths have comprehensive test coverage.

> Warning: Addressing high-impact risks by creating test cases increases your Linked coverage, but the risk remains active until those tests are verified in CI/CD.
