# Review and approve test cases

Every test case produced by Testnexa enters your suite in a **Pending** status. Test cases remain pending until a team member reviews the steps, validates the assertions, and explicitly approves or rejects them.

> Note: Test cases in the pending state are clearly marked across your suite views. Approving a test case promotes it to active status, which qualifies it for export and pipeline verification.

## Review lifecycle and steps

Follow these steps to triage and approve your generated cases:

1. **Open the Test Cases view**
   Navigate to **Test Cases** or open the specific suite detail view. Filter the list using the status selector set to **Pending**.
2. **Inspect preconditions and execution steps**
   Expand the test case drawer to review preconditions, individual step inputs, and expected postconditions.
3. **Edit or customize steps**
   If an assertion needs project-specific fixture data or selector adjustments, click **Edit** and refine the text inline.
4. **Approve or reject the case**
   Click **Approve** to accept the test case into your active suite, or **Reject** to archive it from exports.
5. **Batch approve**
   Use the multi-select table checkboxes to approve high-confidence test suites in bulk after performing sampling checks.

## Expected result

Approved test cases reflect an active status badge, are included in automated Playwright and Cypress exports, and contribute directly toward your Verified coverage readiness score.

## Guidance & best practices

> Tip: Prioritize reviewing edge boundary and security cases first. These scenarios frequently highlight uncovered business logic in your requirements.

> Warning: Rejected test cases are excluded from code exports. If you reject a test case that maps to a required acceptance criterion, your Linked coverage percentage will decrease accordingly.
