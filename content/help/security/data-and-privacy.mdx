# Data handling and privacy

Testnexa is built for enterprise engineering teams with strict compliance and privacy requirements. We follow a strict principle of data minimization across every stage of generation and storage.

> Note: Testnexa is completely stateless during generation. Nothing is stored in your database until a suite is successfully produced and saved. Unsaved drafts, incomplete prompts, and temporary generation sessions are never recorded.

## What is stored vs what is sent

### 1. What is stored in your workspace
When a test suite is confirmed and saved, Testnexa stores:
- User profile information (email, workspace membership, role).
- Generated suite titles, requirement summaries, and acceptance criteria tags.
- Test cases, steps, assertions, and approval review statuses.
- Audit logs tracking team edits and suite deletion timestamps.

### 2. What is sent to AI model providers
During generation, the requirement text you provide is transmitted securely over TLS to our vetted LLM providers solely to produce the test suite. Under our enterprise agreements:
- **Zero data retention**: Prompts and outputs are discarded immediately after inference.
- **No model training**: Your requirements, code snippets, and test cases are never used to train or fine-tune public models.

## Security architecture

1. **Encryption in transit and at rest**
   All communications use TLS 1.3. Databases and backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256.
2. **Tenant isolation**
   Row-Level Security (RLS) guarantees complete workspace isolation. Users can only access resources belonging to their authorized workspace.
3. **Short-lived credentials**
   All third-party integrations (such as the Testnexa GitHub App) operate via ephemeral, rotating installation tokens.

## Expected result

Your proprietary product requirements remain confidential and secure, fully compliant with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR enterprise standards.

## Guidance & best practices

> Tip: Avoid pasting production passwords, live API tokens, or actual customer PII into requirement descriptions. Use placeholder variables like `{{API_KEY}}` or `user@example.com`.

> Warning: Deleting a test suite or workspace permanently purges its test cases, coverage metrics, and associated audit records from active databases.
